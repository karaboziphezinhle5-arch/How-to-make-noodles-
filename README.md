  # How to make noodles
  <p>The proper steps to make your noodles</p>
  
  ## Ingredients
  <ul><li>A single pack of noodles</li>
       <li>Yellow powder</li>
       <li>Spice</li>
       <li>250ml of water</li>
  </ul>
  
  ## Instructions
  <ol><li>You should boil your water</li>
      <li>Pour it into a pot</li>
      <li>Add your noodles</li>
      <li>Add your noodle spice and a teaspoon of yellow powder</li>
     <li>Wait for 4-5 minutes and then seve</li>
     <li>Serve warm matybe with cheese, polony or viennas etc.</li>
   </ol>
